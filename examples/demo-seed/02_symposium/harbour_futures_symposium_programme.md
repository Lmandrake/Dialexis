Harbour Futures Annual Symposium, 20 February 2026, Pellow Bay
Hosted by the Brackenmoor Foundation.

Ambrose Merriweather (program officer, Brackenmoor Foundation) opened the day by reviewing the
Harbour Futures Program, which funds four coastal projects:
  - Kestrel Array, a network of moored sensor buoys (Tidal Sensing Group, Halloway Institute;
    lead Ysolde Marchbank)
  - Orrery Tide Model, an ensemble tide forecast (Coastal Modelling Group; lead Odalys Ferncastle)
  - Flood Atlas, a public flood-risk map (Quillfeather University; lead Zillah Morrow)
  - Seawall Census, a survey of harbour defences (Pellow Bay Harbour Authority; lead Delphine Ruskin)

Seraphine Lucan announced that Tamsin Ashdown (Halloway Institute) holds this year's Brackenmoor
Early Career Fellowship.

Lisbeth Arkwright (Quillfeather University) spoke on ensemble tide forecasting.
